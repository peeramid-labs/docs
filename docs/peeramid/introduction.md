# Introduction

Peeramid network was founded with the mission to create a social protocol that enables anyone in the world to create communities from scratch, by simply defining an idea and enabling bottom-up, progressive decentralization of their concept into a diverse and healthy organization representing a community of likeminded individuals, capable of delivering goods and services.

This framework can be seen as superset, accommodating any existing decentralized or centralized organization or community system within a system that can enable communities with a variety of new tools and methods to improve efficiency and effectiveness. Facilitating consensus-building. Discovering and validating ideas

The internet has facilitated global economic growth for the past 30 years. Distributed ledger technology and financial market instruments have further opened up the global marketplace, making it exceptionally competitive.
Online labor platforms have emerged to connect service providers who are unable to fight off the marketing competitions as this requires specialized tech skills, on their own, with customers and ensure high quality of service. However, these platforms don't allow service providers to share in the organization's success. This problem is paramount for decentralized autonomous organizations, which struggle to provide goods and services while remaining decentralized and autonomous.

 Proof of competence is a core concept behind Peeramid Network, which aims to establish agent competence to conduct knowledge work related to specific types of labor that needs to be produced. This can be used to define the relationship between anyone contributing knowledge work and their ability to participate in organizational management and provide their competence related labor without need to have any external approval or attestations.

As for today, Proof of competence is an unsolved problem and is a blocking stone in any meritocratic system design [\[ref\]](https://rbenabou.scholar.princeton.edu/sites/g/files/toruqf3331/files/rbenabou/files/meritocracy_2000.pdf). The problem is even more paramount as AI agentic workflows begin to show ability to replace human labor \[[ref](https://mirror.xyz/peersky.eth/3QmBmkYdlnzinaePefVxMTwhi-vnS1xS8BeAdLtyCvY)\], reliable benchmarking & incentivization mechanisms are needed.

If solved, this would allow for meritocratic organizations to emerge that have the ability to perform with autonomy of yet an unwitnessed level and higher performance efficiency than traditional structures.

Peeramid Network core assumptions are:

* Competition that drives progress can be tamed to streamline continuous improvement of truly autonomous systems in the form of collaboration.
* Measuring knowledge works creates a doorway for measuring one's competence hence allows to create meritocratic organizations and knowledge work markets.
* Today's professional management HR and corporate governance systems are in similar stagnation as the banking system was compared to distributed ledger technology innovation.

The Peeramid social network layer can incorporate any existing chain with assets. While primarily discussed as built on top of Ethereum, it can run in any setup, including private and restricted ones. It stays interoperable by adding two extra tokens on top of any new or existing project.
